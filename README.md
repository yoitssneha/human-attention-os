# Human Attention OS

> **Humans should spend their time and energy on what requires human judgment. AI should handle the rest.**

Read the first-principles vision in [`VISION.md`](VISION.md).

## The vision

AI is not going away. We are entering an era where communication, coordination, and routine execution can increasingly be handled by intelligent systems.

The scarce resource of the future is not information. It is **human attention**.

Human Attention OS is a pre-AGI product for that world: a universal layer that understands a person’s context, handles predictable communication and execution, and brings humans in only when human judgment is genuinely required.

> **Humans spend their time and energy where the human brain should spend it. AI handles the rest.**

## What it does

The system receives communication in natural language—voice or text—and understands the relevant context:

- who is speaking and what they are responsible for;
- the people, teams, projects, relationships, and priorities involved;
- existing conversations, commitments, documents, schedules, and policies;
- what it is allowed to do and when it needs approval.

It then decides whether to:

1. handle the request automatically;
2. communicate with the right people or systems;
3. create, coordinate, and complete the executable action;
4. ask a focused clarification question; or
5. involve a human because judgment, creativity, empathy, accountability, ethics, or ambiguity is required.

## The central principle

This is not another productivity tool for employees. It is an operating layer for anyone living in an AI era.

The goal is not to help people communicate more efficiently. The goal is to remove communication that does not need to happen between humans at all.

Routine messages, reminders, scheduling, status collection, follow-ups, translation, coordination, and predictable workflows should happen in the background. People should see the decisions, exceptions, relationships, and actions that truly need them.

```text
Communication → context → executable action → autonomous follow-through
                                      ↓
                         human attention only for exceptions
```

## The AI-native organization

An AI-native organization is not one where humans disappear. It is one designed around human judgment rather than human coordination overhead.

In that organization:

- AI handles routine communication and operational follow-through;
- humans handle strategy, creativity, empathy, relationships, and consequential judgment;
- predictable work is automated end to end;
- exceptions are surfaced with the context needed to act;
- every commitment has an owner, a deadline, and a path to resolution;
- the system continuously learns how work and decisions move through the organization.

## Why voice and language matter

The interface should be natural enough for everyone: voice, text, and local languages, including code-mixed speech. People should not have to learn a new project-management language to delegate work to the system.

Sarvam is the natural foundation for a Sarvam-native expression of this vision: Indian-language speech recognition, translation, transliteration, language identification, text-to-speech, conversational intelligence, and voice agents can make the attention layer accessible beyond software-native users.

The product is not the models alone. The product is the context, permissions, action layer, and reliable follow-through built on top of them.

## A simple example

> “The customer wants the enterprise demo moved to next week. Check availability, reschedule it, update the relevant record, and tell the account owner.”

The system understands the context, performs the coordination, updates the systems, and only interrupts a human if there is a conflict, uncertainty, or decision that requires judgment.

## The long-term promise

> **Automate communication. Protect human attention. Build organizations around what only humans can do.**

This is a step toward a world where AI does not merely answer questions or generate content. It continuously turns intent into execution—while humans retain responsibility for the choices that matter.
